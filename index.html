Who we are

From an RAF hangar to a second home.

Lagos Country Club was founded on 3rd August 1949 in a former Royal Air Force hangar used by West African Airways, and incorporated on 30th October 1956. Today it is one of Nigeria’s foremost family clubs — a private club where members from every tribe and faith meet as one.

A family club

A private club where three generations spend their weekends together — sport for the young, fellowship for all.

Unity in diversity

A mini-Nigeria: members from almost every ethnic group and faith, gathered as one club.

Seventy-seven years of peace

Steady growth without the crises that have troubled many clubs — a culture members guard closely.

Dining & the Garden Bar

Good food, friendly prices, long evenings.

From ofada rice and peppered snail to international plates, our kitchens serve nourishing local and continental cuisine. Then there’s the Garden Bar — the members’ favourite spot for suya, chilled drinks and conversation that runs late.
